Sol Client's night sky data
===========================

stars.dat
---------
Every star down to magnitude 6.5, with its position (J2000), magnitude, B-V colour index and, for the brightest,
its proper name, taken from the HYG Database v4.1 by David Nash (https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

stars.dat is an adaptation of the HYG Database (cut to magnitude 6.5, reduced to these fields and stored in a
binary layout by tools/sol-assets/make_sky.py) and is shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.
