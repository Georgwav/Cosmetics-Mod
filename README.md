# Sol Client look sheets

Contact sheets for georgwav/cosmetics-mod#31. They come from a local look run on 1.21.11 at 1280×720, with Pixel light (the default).

- `sol-sheet-a.png`, `sol-sheet-b.png`: every theme's showpiece, day and night, each next to the Ember Sun lab frame (the bar).

- `frames/`: the showpieces at full size.

## The night (georgwav/cosmetics-mod#36)

From local look runs on 1.21.11 at 1280×720, after the Night Sky lab. The lab frames come from the lab itself, rendered at the same views.

- `night/lab_sky.png`: the night sky against the lab: south and north at midnight, the zenith, the Milky Way's core (new moon).
- `night/lab_themes.png`: Aurora, Nebula, Monsoon, Silver Night and Grace at night against the lab's.
- `night/theme_nights.png`: every theme's night.
- `night/timelapse_<theme>.png`: each theme through the eight times of day, from one place.
- `night/moon_looks.png`: the moon looks, the crescent with earthshine, the full moon.
- `night/planets.png`: Saturn and Jupiter through a spyglass, the wild worlds overhead, a constellation in focus, the new-moon Milky Way.
- `night/twilight.png`: the Earth's shadow and Belt of Venus, the afterglow, the zodiacal light, airglow.
- `night/events.png`, `night/events.gif`: meteors (a burst, a fireball and its train, an earthgrazer), the comet, a satellite flare, ships jumping, the superlaser; the clip is sixteen frames a fifth of a second apart.
- `night/ui_night_page.png`, `night/ui_about.png`: World › Night sky and Settings › About.
